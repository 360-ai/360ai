# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JavaScript — explicitly selected for the new homepage.

## Users

Decision-makers in small and medium-sized companies who are evaluating AI or automation and need to decide where it has a real business case.

## Product Purpose

360ai creates clarity before a company invests in AI, automation, or a technical implementation. The homepage should qualify and generate first conversations via email or WhatsApp.

## Positioning

360ai begins with process, data, responsibility, and economic value — not with selling a tool or implementation hours. It can recommend preparation work or a conscious no as well as implementation.

## Operating Context

An initial conversation leads into an AI-readiness and potential analysis, then into workshops or implementation support. The business serves companies from Frankenberg (Eder), Waldeck-Frankenberg and beyond.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Primary offer: KI-Readiness- & Potenzialanalyse. Entry point: cost-free, non-binding initial conversation. Contact channels are email and WhatsApp. Public pricing is intentionally omitted. Webdesign remains an additional, non-primary service.

## Brand Commitments

Name: 360ai. The new homepage must be minimalist, clean, modern, with concise German copy, an original hero, and intentional scroll effects. Existing site is an anti-reference for its text-heavy conventional landing-page style.

## Evidence on Hand

Confirmed offering copy lives in `360AI-Angebotsarchitektur.md`. Reference clients named by the user: Schreck, Del Sud, Milele, AVAS, Beloved Dogs. No approved logo files or profile URLs were supplied, so no brand marks or profile destination is fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Erst Klarheit, dann entscheiden, dann umsetzen.
- Make the business problem visible before recommending technology.
- Be direct, precise, and economical with words.
- Earn trust through an honest process rather than generic AI promises.
